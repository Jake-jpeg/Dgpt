## What we collect

- Account identity from your sign-in provider (name, email, a stable
  identifier). We never receive or store your password.
- Information you enter in the structured intake (facts about your
  marriage, property, and related matters) — only after conflict screening
  is completed and an attorney allows your matter to proceed.
- Documents you upload at the firm's request.
- Conflict-screening identities (both parties' names and prior names).
- Your acknowledgment of our relationship disclosure (version + time).
  We do not record your IP address or browser details with that
  acknowledgment unless the firm has expressly enabled it.
- Operational audit records (what happened and when — kept in a
  tamper-evident log; names appear there only as one-way hashes).

## What we do NOT do

- We do not sell or share your information for marketing.
- We do not use your information to train AI models.
- We do not let software make legal decisions about your matter: conflict
  clearance, document approval, and any release of documents are performed
  by an attorney.
- Internal AI assistance, when the firm enables it, is used only by firm
  personnel to organize and draft internal work product; its output is
  always reviewed by an attorney before anything based on it reaches you.

## Retention

If the firm does not take on your matter, or an intake is abandoned, we
delete the substance of what you provided on a schedule set by the firm,
keeping only the minimum needed for professional-responsibility purposes
(conflict-of-interest history, your consent records, and the audit trail).
