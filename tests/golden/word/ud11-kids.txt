NOW, on motion of the Plaintiff, it is
ORDERED AND ADJUDGED that the Referee's Report, if any, is hereby confirmed; and it is

further

ORDERED AND ADJUDGED, that the marriage between Alexandra M.

Sampleton-Vandermeer, Plaintiff, and Christopher J. Sampleton-Vandermeer, Defendant, is

hereby dissolved by reason of the Irretrievable Breakdown of the marriage relationship for a

period of at least six (6) months pursuant to DRL §170(7); and it is further

ORDERED AND ADJUDGED, that there are 2 children of the marriage, namely: Aria

Sampleton-Vandermeer, born March 4, 2019; Beau Sampleton-Vandermeer, born July 9, 2021;

and it is further

ORDERED AND ADJUDGED, that Plaintiff shall have custody of the minor child(ren) of the

marriage, i.e.: Aria Sampleton-Vandermeer (d.o.b. March 4, 2019); Beau

Sampleton-Vandermeer (d.o.b. July 9, 2021); and it is further

ORDERED AND ADJUDGED, that Defendant shall have visitation with the minor child(ren)

of the marriage in accordance with the parties' settlement agreement; and it is further

ORDERED AND ADJUDGED, that there are no court orders with regard to custody or

visitation to be continued; and it is further

ORDERED AND ADJUDGED, that Defendant shall pay to Plaintiff, as and for the support of

the parties' unemancipated child(ren) of the marriage, namely: Aria Sampleton-Vandermeer

(d.o.b. March 4, 2019); Beau Sampleton-Vandermeer (d.o.b. July 9, 2021), the sum of $1,850.00

per month, commencing on September 1, 2026, and to be paid directly to Plaintiff, together with

such dollar amounts or percentages for child care, education and health care as set forth below in

accordance with the parties' Settlement Agreement; and it is further
ORDERED AND ADJUDGED, that, if maintenance is to be paid pursuant to this Judgment of

Divorce, then, subject to the terms of DRL §240(1-b), upon termination of the maintenance

award, the amount of child support payable shall be adjusted, without prejudice to either party's

right to seek a modification pursuant to DRL §236(B)(9)(2); and it is further

ORDERED AND ADJUDGED, that Defendant shall pay to Plaintiff, as and for reasonable

child care expenses pursuant to written agreement of the parties, 40%; and it is further

ORDERED AND ADJUDGED, that Defendant shall pay to Plaintiff, as and for the

non-custodial parent's pro rata share of future health care expenses not covered by insurance,

40% of such expenses; and that the custodial parent provides the health insurance for the

children, and the non-custodial parent's pro rata share of health insurance premiums for the

children is $180.00; and it is further

ORDERED AND ADJUDGED, that Defendant shall pay to Plaintiff, for education or

extraordinary expenses of the children, 40%, pursuant to written agreement of the parties; and it

is further

ORDERED AND ADJUDGED, that a separate Qualified Medical Child Support Order shall be

issued simultaneously herewith; and it is further

ORDERED AND ADJUDGED, that no award of maintenance is made to either party, neither

party having requested maintenance; and it is further

ORDERED AND ADJUDGED, that equitable distribution of marital property is not in issue,

there being no marital property to distribute; and it is further
ORDERED AND ADJUDGED, that compliance with DRL §253 regarding removal of barriers

to remarriage is not required as the parties were married in a civil ceremony; and it is further

ORDERED AND ADJUDGED, that any applications to enforce or modify the provisions of

this Judgment shall be brought in a County wherein one of the parties resides; and it is further

ORDERED AND ADJUDGED, that the parties have been provided notice pursuant to DRL

§255 regarding health care coverage continuation, tax consequences, and other rights affected by

this judgment; and it is further

ORDERED AND ADJUDGED, that this judgment shall be entered and that the marriage of the

parties is dissolved as of the date of entry of this judgment.
